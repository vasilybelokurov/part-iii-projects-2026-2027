## Notes on fit
- **03, 05, and `01_cerny2023_bootesV_discovery`** are the strongest — they show the exact
  data/method. Figure 05 is the project's own premise made visual (clumps in [Fe/H]–H_J);
  the Cerny Boötes V panel is the whole UFD search in one image (isochrone selection →
  spatial overdensity → CMD verification). Use it as Project 1's Fig 1, with NGC 6822 (or
  NGC 6397) as an optional "why Euclid" opener.
- **02** Fig 1 is a **two-panel side-by-side**: S5-HVS1's Gaia-sky trajectory (left) and the
  Euclid NISP grism image (right) — the goal and the data. The Marchetti fast-star phase
  space is a strong supporting figure; the artist impression and the quantitative
  launch-region plot are unused candidates. #4 (a v_Euclid-vs-v_Gaia stellar plot) was
  sought but not available as a clean published figure — the Q1.VII comparison plots are
  galaxy redshift vs DESI, not stellar RV vs Gaia.
- **04** now has four figures telling the project's story: `04a` the focal-plane/40-s
  cadence, `04b` a real intra-transit signal, `04c` the scan-angle systematic to defeat,
  `04d` the short-timescale HR-diagram deliverable. `04a` + `04b` + `04c` are what make
  this distinct from a generic "search Gaia for variables" project.
